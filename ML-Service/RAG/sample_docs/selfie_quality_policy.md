# Selfie Image-Quality Policy

## Purpose

Selfie quality checks determine whether an image is usable for face detection and comparison. They report factors such as blur, brightness, and face count.

## Important limitation

These checks are not liveness or anti-spoofing detection. They cannot determine whether the camera observed a live person rather than a photo, screen replay, or mask.

## Review behavior

Applicants should capture one unobscured face in even lighting and may retake a poor-quality image. Uncertain face similarity is routed to a verifier; low similarity is not an automatic rejection in the Phase 3 policy.
